Core Engine & Training Data Breakdown
🧮
AI Potability Analyzer Nearly 20,000 reference samples
Analyzes up to 12 distinct sensor readings simultaneously. The algorithm mathematically compares your current water's chemical signature to thousands of historical global records to output an accurate safety confidence rating.
📏
Safety Rules & Guidelines 2,100+ Indian monitoring stations
Strictly cross-checks the water against established WHO and BIS environmental guidelines. Runs independent safety checks to flag flood risks, acidic danger, high salinity, plumbing hardness, and specific use suitabilities.
⚗️
Heavy Metal Risk Analyzer 15 specific chemical parameters
Watches out specifically for hazardous microscopic materials like Arsenic, Lead, and excessive Coliform Bacteria. This allows the system to identify exactly where contamination might be originating (e.g. factory runoff vs biological waste).
🌧️
Open-Meteo Weather Integration Live Forecast & Historical Data
Automatically reaches out to global weather satellites to verify rainfall over the last 7 days at your exact map pin. If heavy rain is detected, the AI instantly increases the mathematical danger rating for flood-borne bacteria.
